### Sources

Lists of position histories include acting officers, as well as official officeholders. One date is listed for the start of each officer's holding of the position. In the case that multiple officers began on the same day (which requires that one of them also ended on the same day), the official officeholder will be preferred. The end of holding the position can be considered the same day as, or the day before, the next officer took the position. Because acting officers are included, lists of officers should not be used to determine the Nth holder of an office.
