Hawaii is the only state which has no incorporated places, that is, no official cities. All places in Hawaii are dependent Census-designated places. Honolulu, the capital of Hawaii, is therefore the only state capital in the United States which is not incorporated.

Hawaii commonly uses two demonyms, "Hawaiian" being used for any people who identify as Native Hawaiian and for non-personal descriptions (I.E. Hawaiian Government, Hawaiian Governor), and "Hawaii resident" being used to describe residents of the Hawaiian islands who do not identify as Native Hawaiian. "Hawaiian" is included here, but this should be reevaluated at a later time for accuracy.
