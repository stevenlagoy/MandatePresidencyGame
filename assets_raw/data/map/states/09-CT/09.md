
Towns are free to choose their own government structure. Nineteen of the towns in Connecticut are consolidated city-towns, and Naugatuck is a consolidated borough-town. City incorporation requires a Special Act by the Connecticut General Assembly. All cities in Connecticut are dependent municipalities. Except for Groton, all currently existing cities in Connecticut are consolidated with their parent town.
