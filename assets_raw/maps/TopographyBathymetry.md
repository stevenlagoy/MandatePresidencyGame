### Description

Displays land topography and seafloor bathymetry in shades of blue, cyan, and green. Areas in a
shade of blue or cyan are ocean, and the shade determines the depth of the ocean in that area.
Bathymetric data is presented as 5 discrete depth steps. Topographic data is presented on a
continuum from the darkest color corresponding to the lowest represented elevation to the lightest
color corresponding to the highest represented elevation. Elevations between these extremes are
shaded with a linear interpolation between the lightest and darkest colors. This map is missing
bathymetric data for much of the Pacific ocean, and is missing topographic data for the following
areas: Russia, Greenland, Iceland, Pacific Islands, including Hawaii, Bermuda, Caribbean Islands
including Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands, Belize, and Guatemala.

### Selected Shades

#### Bathymetry:

- `#00FFFF` = Ocean depth of <1m
- `#00C0FF` = Ocean depth of 1-200m
- `#0080FF` = Ocean depth of 201–500m
- `#0040FF` = Ocean depth of 501–2500m
- `#0000FF` = Ocean depth of >2500m

#### Topography:

- `#008000` = Land elevation of -134m
- `#FFFFFF` = Land elevation of 6132m
