
This data was sourced from the United States Census Bureau and United States Department of Transportion.
The boundaries of Congressional Districts are those used in the 119th Congress, and will be innacurate for the 2028 election cycle.

Three areas of the nation, one each in the states of Connecticut, Illinois, and New Hampshire, are officially "not defined".
These are given the geoIDs, respectively, of: 09ZZ, 17ZZ, and 33ZZ. These are all entirely unpopulated unorganized waters within the borders of the United States, without official 'representation' in Congress.
These areas remain colored on this map.
