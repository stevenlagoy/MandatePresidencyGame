### Description

Each physiographic province of North America is displayed in a unique color. The hierarchy of
physiographic regions and provinces for the contiguous United States match the United States
Geological Society's definitions. The hierarchy of physiographic regions and provinces for the
rest of North America are based on Encyclopædia Britannica. Pacific Islands are assigned one
physiographic region, and separate physiographic provinces based on established island chains,
with `Pacific Islands` as a catchall for islands outside an established island chain. Land in
Russia and Iceland is assigned to the `Arctic Plain` physiographic province. Physiographic
provinces in the same region are given similar colors for readability. Color similarity should not
be used as a test for physiographic region membership. Any area shaded black (#000000) is open
water which does not belong to a physiographic region or province. Areas which are not shaded
black (#000000) may not be land (I.E. some Pacific islands are shown as "bubbles" which are larger
than the islands themselves and cover some water). Physiographic region colors are selected
randomly. Physiographic province colors are selected by permuting physiographic region colors and
verifying color selections are unique.
