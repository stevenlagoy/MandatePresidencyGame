### Description

Each county subdivision / third-order subdivision of the United States is displayed in a unique
color. County subdivisions in the same state are given similar colors, and county subdivisions in
the same county-equivalent area are given very similar colors, for readability. Color similarity
should not be used as a test for state or county membership. Only land and water within the states
and territories of the United States are colored, and all other areas are shaded black (#000000);
any pixel which is not black (#000000) is a part of a county subdivision of the United States.
State colors are selected pseudorandomly to ensure sufficient difference in color between
neighboring states to promote readability. County colors are selected by permuting state colors
and verifying color selections are unique. County subdivision colors are selected by permuting
county colors and verifying color selections are unique.
