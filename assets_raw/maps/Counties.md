### Description

Each county-equivalent area / second-order subdivision of the United States is displayed in a
unique color. Counties in the same state or territory are given similar colors for readability,
though color similarity should not be used as a test for state membership. Only land and water
within the states and territories of the United States are shaded, and all other areas are colored
black (#000000). State colors are selected to ensure sufficient difference in color between
neighboring states to promote readability. County colors are selected by permuting state colors and
verifying color selections are unique. Any pixel which is not black (#000000) is a part of a
county-equivalent area of the United States. There are no differently-colored borders or blended
colors between counties.
